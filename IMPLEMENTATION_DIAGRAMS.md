## Summary

### **What Backend Does**
1. ? Accepts author emails in CREATE request
2. ? Stores emails in database
3. ? Returns emails in GET/PATCH responses
4. ? Auto-generates APA citation on approval
5. ? Validates JWT tokens on all protected endpoints

### **What Frontend Does**
1. ? Hide author section in upload form
2. ? Show author section in view form
3. ? Save form data to localStorage
4. ? Restore form data on page load
5. ? Store JWT token in localStorage
6. ? Restore session from token on app load
7. ? Display APA citation when thesis is approved

### **Result**
- ? Users stay logged in even after closing browser
- ? Form data persists on page refresh
- ? Author information is captured and displayed
- ? APA citations are auto-generated and ready to copy
